Privacy Policy

IVAN Rclone Backup is a personal backup application operated by its owner using Rclone and Google Drive.

Data Access

The application requests access to Google Drive solely to create, update, verify and restore personal backup files.

Data Storage

Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. Authentication credentials are stored locally and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share personal data with advertisers or other users.

Data Retention

Backup files and historical versions are retained according to the owner's backup policy.

Access and Deletion

The owner can revoke Google account access and manage or delete stored backups at any time.
